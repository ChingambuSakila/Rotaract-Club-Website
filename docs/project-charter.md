## Project Name

Rotaract Club Website Platform

## Project Vision

To develop a professional, responsive and maintainable web platform that enables Rotaract clubs to establish an online presence, showcase their community impact, communicate with the public, and manage their digital content efficiently.

## Primary Objectives

1. Establish a professional online presence for participating Rotaract clubs.
2. Showcase service projects, achievements and community impact.
3. Provide a centralized system for managing website content.
4. Support multiple Rotaract clubs through a reusable architecture.
5. Enable authorized board members and Public Image Committee members to manage relevant content without modifying source code.

## Target Users

* Members of the public.
* Prospective Rotaractors.
* Registered club members.
* Authorized board members.
* Authorized Public Image Committee members.

## Technology Stack

* Frontend: HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.
* Backend: PHP.
* Database: MySQL.
* Database access: PHP PDO.
* Version control: Git and GitHub.

## Target Launch Date

30 November 2026.

## Development Approach

Iterative development, requirements validation, incremental testing and continuous documentation.

## Initial Scope

Public information pages, events, news and announcements, service projects and impact reports, a contact form, and an administrative dashboard.

## Key Constraint

The project will be developed by one software engineering student, with limited stakeholder participation and a currently undetermined budget.

## Important Assumption

The platform is intended to support multiple clubs. Whether these clubs will share one application and database or receive separate website installations remains to be decided during architecture and requirements analysis.
